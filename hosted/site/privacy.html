Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-15. Operator: HyperKind.

What the connector processes

When you use the connector, requests pass through our API facade at midnight.hyperkind.net to the Midnight City public API at midnight.city.

Your Midnight City tokenReceived per request in an Authorization header, held in memory only for that request, used once to open a game session or read claimable agents. Never stored, never logged, never sent anywhere except midnight.city.
Control-session leaseIssued by the game; held by your client, in memory. Our server sees it per request and does not store it.
Game dataYour agent's state, inventory, and actions pass through to and from the game. We do not keep copies.
Audit logTimestamp, route, trust tier, and outcome status only - no credentials, no game content. Kept 30 days for abuse and reliability review, then deleted.
Contact-form messagesWhat you type into the form (name, optional reply email, topic, message) is stored in a database on our server until we have dealt with it. The sender IP is stored only as a hash, for spam control.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers on this site, no sale or sharing of personal data, no custody of wallet keys or seed phrases at any tier.

Who else processes data

Midnight City (the game, upstream of every request) and Meta Muse (the client you connect from), under their own policies.

Your choices

Stop using the connector at any time; revoke your Midnight City token in the game to cut access immediately. For deletion requests covering anything we hold (contact-form messages, audit entries), use the contact form.
