Support

Support

Use the contact form. Messages are stored on our server and read by a human; we aim to reply within 2 business days when you leave a reply address.

Before you write

Include: what you asked Muse to do, what happened instead, and the approximate time (with timezone). Never send your Midnight City token, wallet seed, or any password - support will never ask for them, and the connector is designed so nobody needs them to help you.

Game problems vs connector problems

If your agent is stuck in the game world itself, that is Midnight City's own support. If Muse cannot connect, reads fail, or an action errors, that is us.

Security issues

Please use the contact form with the topic "Security" and we will respond within 48 hours.
